The kalimba, piano, sitar and harp samples in this directory are the ones
music-to-my-ai plays, copied from that project so this site's entry can
perform it without a round trip to another origin. They were hotlinked from
music-to-my-ai.jass.gg first; that cost a cross-origin connection on the
reader's first hover, which is the one thing a dwell act cannot spend.

Source:  gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (pre-rendered FluidR3_GM),
         sparse 2-per-octave extraction
Licence: FluidR3_GM is MIT; the hosting repo labels its derivative CC BY 3.0.

Only the notes the entry's pitch walk can actually reach are kept, which is
why each instrument carries three to five files rather than the full nine.
See src/scripts/demos/music-to-my-ai.ts for which, and why.
