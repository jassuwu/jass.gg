## Question

Which entries can perform their own trick, in the list, at portfolio quality —
and what is each one's honest five-second demo?

## The decision already made (do not reopen)

jass ruled it in ticket 11's resolution: aliveness lives **in the entry
list**, not in a new section — "let's not pollute the page by just adding a
section for this. let's just do it along the showcase of all the works." The
list is the shelf; touching a toy makes it do its trick.

This is also the map's substance-signals answer in its strongest form: proof
by performance. A one-line entry that does its trick when touched says more
about quality than any stat — and ticket 17 handles the numbers side.

## Rules

- **Design from ticket 12's findings only.** The pass-four sketches (the
  oneko cat, the cursor swaps, mojify-ing the description, the vergil slice,
  the quilt bloom, the donkey on the underline) are candidate shapes, several
  known to be wrong — jass said so. Reality first.
- **The demo is the product's own trick** — if the honest demo would need to
  be built from scratch at higher quality than the product, the entry stays
  plain. jass's bar: only good if the products are of the quality the
  portfolio expects.
- **Uneven coverage is fine.** These are eggs; a plain row is not a failure.
- **The grammar comes from ticket 14** — dwell on pointer, whatever touch
  translation it settled, no repeats, reduced-motion rule inherited.
- **Prefer the project's own assets** (ticket 12 item 5) over reimplementation.
- Passes, not pitches: build a batch, jass kills what's noise.

## Settled (grilling, aug 15)

- **Pass one builds the certain tier**: quilt (embed the live SVG endpoint),
  onandemo (the real published engine, the real cat), liquid-glass-cursor
  (the real npm script; Chromium-only, nothing on Firefox/Safari).
- **savemefrom is upgraded from in-row demo to full-page gag**, in jass's
  words: reimplement what savemefrom does, on this portfolio — click/tap the
  row's description and *this page* gets judgment-cut, slices, and comes back
  whole. **Repeatable** (a deliberate click is consent), lazy-loaded on first
  intent, the vergil clip re-encoded smaller than savemefrom's 15.2 MB
  original. IP stance from jass: "i don't really think it matters."
- **mojify: only if embedding its real render earns its place on screen** —
  jass: "we can generate and embed something if needed, but let's not force
  it."
- **The no-performance rows stay plain, firmly** (skills, subway-cursors,
  better-splitwise, andrew-dictate). Uneven-ness is what makes the performing
  rows feel found instead of installed. If the plain rows feel dead once pass
  one ships, the fallback is depth-in-writing for the `things` — never
  manufactured filler details.

## Done when

Every entry has either a working demo jass kept or an explicit "stays plain"
with the reason.
