# The 404 brainrot

Type: prototype (HITL)
Status: open
Blocked by: nothing — buildable now

## Question

The page for people who are lost plays the video people watch while lost.
Does it survive contact with the actual page?

## The decision already made (do not reopen)

jass killed the "404 remembers old pages" idea outright: "don't do the old
pages. just forget about them or location.pathname shit. **404 is 404.**" No
path echo, no murmurs about dead routes, no redirect nostalgia. The brainrot
is the idea that lives.

## To settle

1. **Which footage.** A copyrighted subway surfers clip is a rights problem
   on a page indexed forever; candidates that dodge it: jass's own
   subway-cursors assets doing a run, a self-recorded minecraft parkour loop,
   anything he owns. Ticket 12's asset inventory may answer this.
2. **The form.** Small, muted, looping, machine-framed — a monitor in the
   corner of the error, not a hero. The page's one full accent stays on the
   joke ("pls no one talks to me 😔"); the video must not dethrone it.
3. **JS-off / reduced-motion / data cost.** JS-off gets today's 404, whole.
   A visitor on mobile data hitting a dead link should not download megabytes
   of brainrot uninvited — figure out the polite version (poster + tap to
   play? tiny loop?).
4. **Does it read as the friend** — leaning over on the worst page with
   something dumb to watch — **or as decoration?** Judge on screen.

## Settled (grilling, aug 15)

- **Footage: generic gameplay off youtube** — jass: "just get some off of yt.
  it's just gameplay." IP stance: "some general subway surfers gameplay
  video and minecraft speedrun video, some satisfying video generic brainrot
  should definitely be okay."
- **Form: a small curated set, picked at random per visit, lazy-loaded** —
  jass: "lazy load random curated brainrot." Muted, cropped small, compressed
  hard (~1–2 MB per loop), machine-framed like a monitor in the corner,
  poster-with-tap under data-saver. The page's one full accent stays on the
  joke line.

## Done when

jass has seen it on the real 404 and kept or killed it.
