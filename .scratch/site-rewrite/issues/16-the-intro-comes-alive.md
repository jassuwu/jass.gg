# The intro comes alive

Type: prototype (HITL)
Status: open
Blocked by: 14 (the friend under a thumb); the marginalia half also by 20
(jass's pairs)

## Question

Which beats in the intro get a performance, and do they land as the friend or
as a soundboard?

## The two halves

**Words performing themselves** — jass: "100% agree." The standing rule: the
copy leads, the trick follows; never write copy to enable a trick. Candidates
already in the copy, ranked:

1. **"agents"** — the best beat available on the page. Dwell on the word and
   a second cursor appears and starts doing things by itself: selects a
   nearby sentence, hovers a link, maybe retypes a word slightly wrong and
   fixes it. "im excited about & scared of my agents" — demonstrated, deadpan,
   nothing explaining it.
2. **"toys"** — the section label is itself a toy: poke the letters and they
   wobble on spring physics. The word performing its own meaning, on the
   heading that announces the section where everything else performs.
3. Hold everything further ("scared" trembling, etc.) — three performances in
   one paragraph is a soundboard. Two may already be. Judge on screen.

**The marginalia beats** — jass: "yep." Meme reveals on intro words, with the
commentary-editor's discipline: dwell-timed, rare (maybe two in the whole
intro), chosen like an editor chooses the beat. Mechanics per ticket 11's
settlement (pure CSS/JS reveal, unmarked words, margin exists only on desktop
— the touch story now comes from ticket 14 instead of tabindex hacks).
Blocked on jass's (word, meme) pairs — the word is voice, the meme is taste,
both his (ticket 20).
